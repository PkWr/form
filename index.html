About the downloads

An SVG is a drawing that stays sharp at any size. This one is animated and plays in a web browser. A GIF is a looping picture for social media, in the shape chosen above. A video is recorded as the animation plays, so it takes as long as it runs: MP4 where your browser can make it, otherwise WebM.

The contact sheet is an SVG set out at its real size on a portrait page, A3 to A0. Open it in a web browser to look at it, or in design software such as Illustrator, Affinity, Inkscape or Figma to change it or to print it.

Imposed puts two A5 pages on each SRA4 landscape sheet (320 by 225 mm) in booklet order, with 3 mm of bleed and crop marks: print double sided at full size, flip on the short edge, fold, bind and trim to the marks. Pages gives the plain A5 pages for reading on screen.
